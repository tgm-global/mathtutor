How to Choose the Right Online Math Tutor for Your Child

By Shaifali Jain · 05 October 2026

Searching for an online math tutor can feel overwhelming. There are many options, and every tutor promises better marks. These six checks will help you decide with confidence.

1. Check that the tutor knows your curriculum

CBSE, ICSE, American Common Core, UK GCSE and Australian syllabi all order topics differently and test in different ways. A tutor who has taught your board will know what the school expects and how exam questions are framed.

2. Look for one-on-one attention

In a group class, a child who is confused often stays quiet. In a one-on-one lesson, the tutor sees exactly where the student gets stuck and can change the explanation on the spot.

3. Ask how the first class works

A good tutor starts by understanding the child: current grade, weak topics, school marks and goals. If a tutor jumps straight into teaching without asking questions, that is a warning sign.

4. See how progress is tracked

You should know what is improving and what still needs work. Ask about practice tests, homework review and regular updates for parents.

5. Make sure timings suit you

Families living abroad need a tutor who can work across time zones. Flexible scheduling means fewer missed classes.

6. Take a free consultation first

Talk to the tutor before you commit. Notice whether your child feels comfortable asking questions. Comfort matters as much as skill.
